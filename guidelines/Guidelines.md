# FindLook Design System: working rules

* The site is bilingual (English default, Português BR). Every user-visible string must exist in both languages: use `const { t, bi } = useI18n()` from `src/app/i18n.tsx` (`t("English", "Português")`). Sidebar labels live in the `ptLabels` map in `src/app/App.tsx`.
* Every addition, change or removal in the design system (new page, component, token, menu change) must be logged as a new entry at the TOP of `src/app/components/showcase/shared/changelog.ts` with both `en` and `pt` text. It renders in the "What's New" section on Home.
* Cards never scroll horizontally (rule of the product owner, 18/11/2026). Content adapts to the width: tables hide less important columns or become a list, tabs wrap to the next line, boards stack their columns on phones, galleries become a wrapping grid. `ui/table`, `ui/tabs` and `AbasComContador` follow this rule.
* Rows with tags or actions (rules of the product owner, 24/11/2026): in grids, text never goes past the tags or actions column. The trailing column keeps its width (auto, shrink-0); the text column is minmax(0,1fr) / min-w-0 flex-1 and wraps or clamps inside it at every width (no col-span-full, flex-basis 100% or grid areas like "res res" under the trailing column). On phones (below 640 px), tags and actions go to their own row below the whole text block by default (decision of the product owner, 27/11/2026: LinhaDeLista `quebraAcoes="estreito"` is the default; opt out with `quebraAcoes={false}`, always below with `quebraAcoes`), or the row becomes a stacked card. Row hover (--row-hover) covers the whole row edge to edge with border-radius 0 (only a row that is itself a standalone card keeps its radius); selected rows have the same shape, in light pink level 1 (--primary-sutil); row focus is an inset outline. Use `ui/linha-de-lista.tsx` (LinhaDeLista). Docs: Data display > Rows with tags and actions.
* Cards side by side always have the same height (rule of the product owner, 27/11/2026). Put them in `GradeDeCards` (`ui/grade-de-cards.tsx`; label + card in `ItemDaGrade`): it stretches the row, keeps footers at the bottom and, when the content difference would leave a large empty area (more than 120 px, or more than 1/3 of the tallest card and at least 64 px), switches to horizontal cards, one per row. Card with an action: divider and button in the bottom-right corner (in the horizontal card the `CardFooter` is a full-width row below the content). Never hand-build a grid of cards with `grid` + `items-start`.
* Keep the Home "Explore" grid and the sidebar groups in `src/app/App.tsx` in sync when pages are added or removed.

## Regra dos rosas claros: selecionado, hover e destaque (decisão da dona do produto, 27/11/2026)

A dona do produto: "o estado selecionado fica com a mesma cor do estado hover [o rosa do meio]. E o hover passa a ser cinza claro", e "isso vale para o FindLook também".

- **Selecionado** `--primary-suave` #FFECF0 (o rosa do meio): item ativo do menu, chip, segmento e linha selecionados, botão "✓ Selecionado", plano escolhido. Selecionado com o mouse em cima continua neste rosa.
- **Hover** cinza claro, nunca rosa: `--accent` (graphite-100 #E9E9EC) nos itens de menu, chips e botões, com o texto passando a `--foreground` (17:1); o `--muted` (graphite-50 #F6F6F7) é claro demais sobre o branco (1,08:1) para um item pequeno. Em cards inteiros com texto cinza de apoio (ItemDeAcao, CardDePreco), o hover é o `--muted` com a borda mudando, porque o `--muted-foreground` sobre o `--accent` fica em 4,46:1 e sobre o `--muted` em 5,0:1. Linhas de lista de ponta a ponta continuam no `--row-hover` (graphite-50).
- **Destaque** `--primary-muted` #FEDFE6: bases de ícones clicáveis (IconeComBase clicavel; o informativo usa a base cinza), chips de estado, faixas de apoio. Nunca marca seleção.
- `--primary-sutil` (#FFF5F8) deixa de ser usado para seleção (o token continua no tema por compatibilidade).
